# Engineer C: cost of rework

Engineer C said rework is the main cost driver on the coating line. A film that fails inspection has to be stripped and redeposited, which is expensive because the target wafers are sent out. They think a cheaper pre-clean would cut rework, but they have not measured it. They are unsure whether the contamination is coming from the load lock or from the handling tools.
