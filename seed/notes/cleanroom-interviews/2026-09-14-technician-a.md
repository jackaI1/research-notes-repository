# Technician A: logging particle counts

Interviewee said the particle counter logs go into a shared spreadsheet by hand, so a reading is often copied wrong. They said the biggest pain point is waiting for the cleanroom bay to clear before a measurement can be repeated. Every repeat run costs about forty minutes of gowning time. They would like the counter to write its own log file. Next step: ask the equipment team whether the counter can export CSV.
